# MotoVault contributor instructions

Read and follow `AGENTS.md`, `docs/roadmap/ARCHITECTURE-CONTRACT.md`, and the
relevant task and phase in `docs/roadmap/roadmap.json`. The architecture contract
is shared across contributors and assistants. Preserve existing user changes and
follow the roadmap, evidence, design, data, shell and permission rules in
`AGENTS.md`. Do not assume a proposed module or quality gate already exists.
